# Checks

Behaviour scripts, run by hand when the logic they cover changes. The checks of each
stage on the card against `prism_reference` are the presets' (`scripts/run/preset.py`,
`scripts/run/raster_frame.py`, `scripts/run/texture_frame.py`, `scripts/run/field.py`,
`scripts/run/apply.py`; [scripts](../scripts/README.md)).

| Script | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| `repo_rules.py` | import direction between packages, file length, ASCII source, in-function imports |
| `raster_model/` (`python -m checks.raster_model` from the repository's root; no card) | the raster stage's middle pass in C (`stages/raster/thread/`) built with host gcc and run on the model's counters of `tiny` and Sponza's overview (5, 120 and 240 fill cores), on seeded hot tiles and at 8,160 tiles, against `model.middle`; `setup.h`'s sizes built with g++ at ten capacities against `model.numbers.sizes`; the model's frame pages (`frame_of`, `decode_frame`) against the reference's plain frame of `tiny`, `s10k` and Sponza's overview; SETUP at the middle pass's slots against `build_lists` and the reference's binning; `setup.boxes` against `setup`'s boxes and `fill.tile_counts` against `bin_triangles`' counts on `tiny`, `s10k`, Sponza's overview (also at 1,024 x 1,024 and 1,920 x 1,080) and Sponza under each DEPTH camera of the shadow host, `corners` against its form before the positions' split (each view, and moved in so the near plane cuts); `kept_counts` twice on Sponza's overview at 1,920 x 1,080 (the second loaded from the kept file) against `tile_counts(boxes(...))`, and another camera another key and file; exit 1 on a difference; `$PRISM_DATA`, `$PRISM_BUILD` |
| `dispatch/` (`python -m checks.dispatch` from the repository's root; no card) | the tile pull's choice (`src/prism/program/dispatch/c/dispatch_choose.c`) built with host gcc and asked by a model of cores with random costs, one thread and four at once: under each policy (with preference, in order, taken once and kept) every record is handed exactly once; with preference and in order no core is refused while a record is left; kept, a core gets only its own slices; exit 1 on a difference |
| `thread_model/` (`python -m checks.thread_model` from the repository's root; no card) | the tile threads' layout in numpy (`stages/raster/model/threads.py`: slices, items and bands, each core's positions, the records a slice in order of weight, the slices' words and places) on `tiny`, `s10k` in three layers and Sponza's overview at 1,920 x 1,080: every item's list as a multiset against the reference's binning, every slot written once, a random log of 120 cores giving the same items and multisets, a heavy tile's bands covering its rows once, the places a running sum; exit 1 on a difference; `$PRISM_DATA` |
| `frame_build/` (`python -m checks.frame_build <tile thread module:NAME> [--kinds ...] [--layers ...] [--defines ...]` from the repository's root; Komorebi's mock build, no card; `PRISM_DATA` set) | the handler `work` (`src/prism/program/frame/`) composed from a tile thread and kinds of thread given by dotted path, its class made and its kernels built for the five CPUs of a core; `toy:TOY` and `toy:WALK` are threads of the check's own (a list pass, an ask, an output with its convert, kept state, a begin, a code variant) that build the loop without any stage's step |
| `camera_moves/` (`python -m checks.camera_moves --pipeline <preset> [--against <stage-wise preset>] --scene <scene> --view <view> [--size W H] --card <card>` from the repository's root) | a preset loaded once and moved out of the load's view by the viewer's pose math (a turn, looks up and down to 80 degrees, a walk ahead, a turn there, a walk to the right, a walk back; looks after each): each frame's code, its reloads (their code by name and seconds) and its live recoveries (what grew, milliseconds), its seconds; with `--against`, each picture against the stage-wise preset's of the same camera byte for byte; the record under `$PRISM_CACHE/camera_moves/`; exit 1 on a reload, a code or a differing picture |
| `threads_frame/` (`python -m checks.threads_frame model\|tile\|depth\|geometry\|whole\|update\|during --pipeline <stage-wise preset> --scene <scene> [--view <view>] [--cores 1 all] [--policy ...] [--heavy N] --card <card>` from the repository's root; `model` needs no card) | the threads' form of a preset against its stage-wise form: `tile` the tile pull alone on the model's records, slices and lists, the picture byte for byte; `depth` the sun's view alone as a DEPTH view on the model's lists (a map of one quadrant: `--shadow-side`, `--sun`), the map's pages byte for byte against the stage-wise one; `update` the field's update as the kept graph (the field's pages it writes against the stage-wise update's, word for word, then the frames after it); `during` frames sent while the update runs, each equal to the stage-wise picture before the update or after it, never a mix; `geometry` the count pull, the sums and the place pull, their records, slices, positions and lists against the model (`stages/raster/model/threads.py`); `whole` the threads' frame's picture, with the first differing tile, its record and the core that ran it; at each count of cores and each way of handing out. On a code or a hang it prints where the frame stood: the frame thread's mark, each core's gave-up word by name, the view's times. `model` checks the checks' own parts against the model |
| `scene_description/` (`python -m checks.scene_description [--files <folder>] [--box <Box.glb>]`; no card) | the scene description and the glTF importer: round trips of a `.glb` and a `.gltf` the check writes under `$PRISM_CACHE` (every attribute, accessor component type and type, strided and normalized views, sparse accessors, the node tree, instancing, lights, cameras, every material field, the sidecar and the instance table), `flatten` against numpy, each refusal; with `--files`, each `.glb` of a folder loaded, checked and its counts (instanced nodes over the whole file) against `<name>.json`, with load seconds and peak memory; with `--box`, Khronos' Box; exit 1 on a difference ([scene description](../docs/concepts/scene_description.md)) |
| `artist_export/` (`python -m checks.artist_export <folder>`; no Blender, no card) | a scene written by `scripts/blender/export.py` against its `stats.json`: every name of the instance table a node with a mesh in the `.glb`, the meshes' triangles times their copies against the drawn triangles and their sum against the stored triangles, with the sidecar's recorded drops (`not_exported.triangles`) added back and each one matching the `.glb`, the sidecar's area lights against the AREA lights, its render camera a camera node of the `.glb`, version 1; one JSON line, exit 1 on a difference ([docs/guides/artist_scenes.md](../docs/guides/artist_scenes.md)) |
| `gltf_scene/` (`python -m checks.gltf_scene`; no card) | the conversion of a glTF file into a scene of the tables (`prism_reference/scene/artist/`) on a `.glb` it writes under `$PRISM_CACHE`: each row of the material mapping and a material falling to each later row (kind, base, slots, exponent, specular, transmission), a textured quad with a texture transform whose corners, looked up in the written texels by `texel_xy`, give the colours glTF puts there, the files read by `load_mesh` and `load_materials` (keys, types, lengths), `make_view` of the view against the file's camera (eye, forward axis, focal length, near, relative 1e-5), the entry's keys and types against Sponza's; `open_scene` twice on one file (the second converts nothing, the same name) and with a changed sidecar (another name), the lights with `visibility="open"` (1 a vertex), the same with `shadow="card"` (the camera, texel and side `shadow="host"` gives, a 1 x 1 map of 0), then replaced by `"bake"`; `layer_blocks` twice on the written scene, unlit and lit (the second read from the kept files, every array equal byte for byte to a fresh computation) and a changed uv value giving another key; exit 1 on a difference ([glTF scenes](../docs/guides/gltf_scenes.md)) |
| `described_light/` (`python -m checks.described_light`; no Blender, no card) | the reference's direct light of a described scene (`prism_reference/direct/described/`) on a written scene of a floor, an occluder and one light of each kind, each against its closed form: a point light's inverse square and cosine, a spot's cone, a sun's cosine, an area light's form factor (1 %), a uniform world's irradiance, the shadow's edge; exit 1 on a difference ([described light](../docs/internals/reference/described_light.md)) |
| `surface/` (`python -m checks.surface`; no Blender, no card) | the reference's surface (`prism_reference/material/`: `brdf`, `filter`, `tangents`, `surface`): the model's reciprocity (1e-12), the white furnace with the view along the normal (base 1, metallic 0 and 1, roughness 0.1 to 1, 64 x 256 cosine-weighted points, at most 1 + 1e-3; the integrals at 30, 60 and 80 degrees printed, not judged), D over the projected hemisphere (roughness 0.2 to 1, 1e-3), `f0` and `diffuse` at their ends; the filters on a constant texture, a 2 x 2 checker (one texel, the whole texture), a ramp under an anisotropic footprint of ratio 8 (1 / 255), each wrap mode past both edges, level 0 of an sRGB texture; the tangents of a quad, mirrored and without normals; every plane at chosen pixels of a textured, normal-mapped quad written as a `.glb` under `$PRISM_CACHE`, against values computed in the check; exit 1 on a difference ([surface](../docs/internals/reference/surface.md)) |
| `apps/prism_viewer/server/check.py` (`python -m prism_viewer.server.check --pipeline lit --scene sponza --view overview`, on card 0 by default; `raster`, `textured`, `lit`, `textured_threads`, `lit_threads`; an opened glTF scene with `--view camera`) | the viewer's server without a browser: 403 without the token on every route, state and catalog (the pipeline, every threads' preset, the scene and its view listed), poses and their frames from the stream, two sizes (one a reload), refusals, each control the loop lists moved and moved back at the view's own pose, the stop by SIGTERM, then each frame's JPEG against the loop's picture through the same encoder (mean absolute difference at most 0.5; another pose must fail it) and the loop's picture by the preset's JUDGE (numbers reported); exit 1 on a failure (`docs/guides/viewer.md`) |
