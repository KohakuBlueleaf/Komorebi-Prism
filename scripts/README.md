# Scripts

Entry points, by purpose: `run/`, `data/`, `blender/`. Each script prints its options with `--help`.

| folder | what it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `run/` | runs on one card, each printing one JSON line and writing its record through `prism.check.harness`: `preset.py` (a preset of `configs/pipeline/` and its check; its options in [docs/guides/cli.md](../docs/guides/cli.md), "The preset runner"); `raster_frame.py` (`--scene` (default `tiny`) `[--view V] [--size W H] [--cores N] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: a whole frame by the raster class alone, stepped by the host, against the reference; the record to `measurements/raster_frame/` by default); `texture_frame.py` (`--scene` (default `sponza`) `--view` (default `overview`) `[--size W H] [--cores N] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: a shaded frame by the raster and texture classes alone, stepped by the host, against the reference's shaded frame; `measurements/texture_frame/` by default); `field.py` (`--scene S [--bounces N] [--updates N] [--stepped] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: the preset `field`, the indirect field's update alone, its check whole or stepped (the host's apply between the steps); `measurements/field_update/runs/` by default); `apply.py` (`--scene sponza\|bistro_interior [--calls N] [--frame-compute] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: the preset `apply`, the light-transport apply alone, `--frame-compute` made with the frame program's compute set; `measurements/apply/` by default). `account.py` (`--pipeline P --scene S [--view V] [--size W H] [--frames N] [--card N] [--trace N] [--out FILE]`: the account of a preset's frames, each command's cores, spread, bytes, counts and closure by `prism.check.account` (not through the harness); `measurements/account/<scene>_<view>_<W>_<H>_<pipeline>.json` by default, its summary as the JSON line); `account_fit.py` (`[--folder FOLDER] [--out FILE]`: the per-item constants of SHADE, LIGHT and SHOW from the account's records, no card; `measurements/account/fit.json` by default); `lookup_model.py` (`--scene S [--view V] --size W H [--frames N] [--terms indirect] [--cores N] [--out FILE]`: the pages a frame's lookups read (textures, the shadow map, the field), modelled on the host from the reference, no card, checked against the account's record of the case; `measurements/lookup_model/<scene>_<view>_<W>_<H>.json` by default). `--card` is 0 by default; an option left out is the preset's |
| `data/` | `generated_scenes.py OUT [NAME ...]` (writes the generated scenes, all four if none named, as `OUT/<name>.npz`, and prints their sizes and checksums); `public_scenes.py list\|fetch\|materials\|lighting\|indirect [SCENE ...] [--data FOLDER] [--workers N] [--rebake]` (the public scenes: list, download and convert, their materials, lighting and indirect files under `$PRISM_DATA` or `--data`; [docs/concepts/scenes.md](../docs/concepts/scenes.md)); `artist_scenes.py list\|fetch\|measure\|export\|render [KEY ...] [--data FOLDER] [--frame N] [--size W H] [--samples N] [--engines CYCLES EEVEE] [--timeout SECONDS]` (the artists' scenes: list, download and unpack, then the scripts of `blender/` into `$PRISM_DATA/artist/<key>/`; [docs/guides/artist_scenes.md](../docs/guides/artist_scenes.md)); `gltf_scene.py convert FILE --name NAME [--camera NODE] [--most-triangles N] [--licence TEXT] [--attribution TEXT]\|report FILE` (a glTF file into a scene of the tables: its mesh and materials files under `$PRISM_DATA` or `--data`, its entry added to `$PRISM_SCENES_EXTRA`, the report as JSON; `report` prints the description's coverage; [docs/guides/gltf_scenes.md](../docs/guides/gltf_scenes.md)) |
| `blender/` | run inside Blender (`blender --background <file.blend> --python scripts/blender/<name>.py -- <arguments>`), each printing one JSON line: `measure.py OUT.json` (what a scene holds), `export.py OUT_STEM [--frame N]` (the `.glb`, its sidecar, its instance table, the world's map), `render.py OUT_STEM --engine CYCLES\|EEVEE --size W H [--samples N] [--frame N]` (the scene's own camera and colour management); `common.py` their shared helpers; [docs/guides/artist_scenes.md](../docs/guides/artist_scenes.md) |
