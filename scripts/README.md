# Scripts

Entry points, by purpose: `run/`, `data/`, `blender/`. Each script prints its options with `--help`.

| folder | what it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `run/` | runs on one card, each printing one JSON line and writing its record through `prism.check.harness`: `preset.py` (a preset of `configs/pipeline/` and its check; its options in [docs/guides/cli.md](../docs/guides/cli.md), "The preset runner"); `raster_frame.py` (`--scene` (default `tiny`) `[--view V] [--size W H] [--cores N] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: a whole frame by the raster class alone, stepped by the host, against the reference; the record to `measurements/raster_frame/` by default); `texture_frame.py` (`--scene` (default `sponza`) `--view` (default `overview`) `[--size W H] [--cores N] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: a shaded frame by the raster and texture classes alone, stepped by the host, against the reference's shaded frame; `measurements/texture_frame/` by default); `field.py` (`--scene S [--bounces N] [--updates N] [--stepped] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: the preset `field`, the indirect field's update alone, its check whole or stepped (the host's apply between the steps); `measurements/field_update/runs/` by default); `apply.py` (`--scene sponza\|bistro_interior [--calls N] [--frame-compute] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: the preset `apply`, the light-transport apply alone, `--frame-compute` made with the frame program's compute set; `measurements/apply/` by default). `account.py` (`--pipeline P --scene S [--view V] [--size W H] [--frames N] [--card N] [--trace N] [--out FILE]`: the account of a preset's frames, each command's cores, spread, bytes, counts and closure by `prism.check.account` (not through the harness); `measurements/account/<scene>_<view>_<W>_<H>_<pipeline>.json` by default, its summary as the JSON line); `threads_account.py` (`--scene S --way stagewise\|order\|prefer\|kept --want PICTURE.npy --card N --out FILE [--pipeline P] [--view V] [--size W H] [--frames N] [--account N] [--trace N] [--heavy N] [--sun AZ EL] [--keep-lists]`: one way of handing out a run, the stage-wise preset (`stagewise`, its first picture written to `--want`) or a tile pull's choice of the threads' preset (each picture judged against `--want`); the frame's and each phase's time, of WHOLE frames (every frame counts and places) unless `--keep-lists` lets the frames after the first stand; [docs/performance/account.md](../docs/performance/account.md)); `waterfall.py` (`RECORD.json --out FRAME.svg [--frame N] [--view V] [--table FILE.md] [--width PX] [--note TEXT]`, no card: one frame of a threads' preset drawn from `threads_account.py`'s record run with `--account N` (and `--trace N` for the sums and the job's applies): a row a core by rank, each thread a bar coloured by kind (count, place, a tile thread's steps), the X280 frame's phases and its answers by port, the job thread's applies, one time axis in ms; each phase's core-ms working and standing, its longest thread and the X280's share as one JSON line); `light_compare.py` (`passes\|compare\|table [KEY ...] [--data FOLDER] [--size W H] [--samples N] [--timeout SECONDS] [--runs RUN ...]`, no card: the reference's direct light of an artist's scene against Cycles' direct-light passes, the pictures and `compare.json` under `$PRISM_DATA/artist/<key>/light/`; [docs/internals/reference/described_light.md](../docs/internals/reference/described_light.md)); `surface_compare.py` (`KEY [KEY ...] [--data FOLDER] [--size W H] [--factor N]`, no card: the reference's surface of an artist's scene against Cycles' planes of it, the pictures and `compare.json` under `$PRISM_DATA/artist/<key>/surface/`; [docs/internals/reference/surface.md](../docs/internals/reference/surface.md)); `account_fit.py` (`[--folder FOLDER] [--out FILE]`: the per-item constants of SHADE, LIGHT and SHOW from the account's records, no card; `measurements/account/fit.json` by default); `lookup_model.py` (`--scene S [--view V] --size W H [--frames N] [--terms indirect] [--cores N] [--out FILE]`: the pages a frame's lookups read (textures, the shadow map, the field), modelled on the host from the reference, no card, checked against the account's record of the case; `measurements/lookup_model/<scene>_<view>_<W>_<H>.json` by default); `compare.py` (`SCENE --out FOLDER [--size W H] [--samples N] [--camera NODE] [--crop NAME ...\|all\|NAME=x0,y0,x1,y1] [--no-reference] [--card C [--pipeline P] [--frames N] [--bake none\|wait]] [--gpu G] [--cache FOLDER]`: a glTF file (or an opened scene) against Cycles reading the same file, the truth kept once a file, camera, size and samples; the reference's direct light by pass and, with `--card`, a preset's picture (by `compare_card.py`); the numbers by pass, one strip, named crops (`compare_crops.py`), all under `--out`; [docs/guides/compare.md](../docs/guides/compare.md)). `--card` is 0 by default; an option left out is the preset's |
| `data/` | `generated_scenes.py OUT [NAME ...]` (writes the generated scenes, all four if none named, as `OUT/<name>.npz`, and prints their sizes and checksums); `public_scenes.py list\|fetch\|materials\|lighting\|indirect [SCENE ...] [--data FOLDER] [--workers N] [--rebake] [--visibility open\|bake] [--shadow host\|card] [--transport dense\|sketch\|cross] [--far-only]` (the public scenes and the opened glTF scenes: list, download and convert, their materials, lighting (`--shadow card` leaves the sun's map to the card) and indirect files (`--transport`: the dense build, or block by block by randomized ranges or by cross approximation; `--far-only` no near block; [docs/performance/transport_build.md](../docs/performance/transport_build.md)) under `$PRISM_DATA` or `--data`; [docs/concepts/scenes.md](../docs/concepts/scenes.md)); `artist_scenes.py list\|fetch\|measure\|export\|render\|planes\|gltf_truth\|selftest [KEY ...] [--data FOLDER] [--frame N] [--plain] [--size W H] [--samples N] [--engines CYCLES EEVEE] [--timeout SECONDS]` (the artists' scenes: list, download and unpack, then the scripts of `blender/` into `$PRISM_DATA/artist/<key>/`; `--plain` exports without the material rewrite; `gltf_truth` renders the exported `.glb` read back by Blender's importer; `selftest` runs the material rewrite's check in Blender; [docs/guides/artist_scenes.md](../docs/guides/artist_scenes.md)); `gltf_scene.py convert FILE --name NAME [--camera NODE] [--most-triangles N] [--licence TEXT] [--attribution TEXT] [--data FOLDER] [--workers N]\|report FILE` (a glTF file into a scene of the tables: its mesh and materials files under `$PRISM_DATA` or `--data`, its entry added to `$PRISM_SCENES_EXTRA`, the report as JSON; `report` prints the description's coverage; [docs/guides/gltf_scenes.md](../docs/guides/gltf_scenes.md)) |
| `blender/` | run inside Blender (`blender --background <file.blend> --python scripts/blender/<name>.py -- <arguments>`), each printing one JSON line: `measure.py OUT.json` (what a scene holds), `export.py OUT_STEM [--frame N] [--plain]` (the `.glb`, its sidecar, its instance table, the world's map; `--plain` leaves the materials as they are), `exportable.py OUT.json [--rays W H]` (what Blender's glTF exporter can carry of the scene, per material: how its base colour, normal, roughness and metallic are written (constant, image, image altered, attribute, lost), alpha, transmission, emission, displacement and volume, each weighted by its drawn triangles, world area and share of the camera's picture (one ray a cell of a W x H grid, 160 x 90 by default); the world, the lights, what Cycles draws and the export does not carry, missing images and libraries, an animated camera), `render.py OUT_STEM --engine CYCLES\|EEVEE --size W H [--samples N] [--frame N]` (the scene's own camera and colour management; Cycles adds the passes of the surface's comparison), `planes.py TRUTH.exr OUT.npz` (run without a `.blend`: the passes of a multilayer EXR as float32 planes, normals and positions +Y up), `from_gltf.py STEM OUT.blend` (run on an empty file: a `.glb` read by Blender's importer, its instance table and sidecar applied, saved as a `.blend`), `light_passes.py FOLDER --size W H [--samples N] [--frame N] [--runs RUN ...]` (Cycles' direct-light passes, every light and a light kind at a time), `selftest_materials.py` (run on an empty file: the material rewrite against values computed here); `common.py`, `graph.py`, `materials.py`, `shaders.py` and `bake.py` their shared helpers; [docs/guides/artist_scenes.md](../docs/guides/artist_scenes.md) |
