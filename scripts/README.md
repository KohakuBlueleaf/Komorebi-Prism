# Scripts

Entry points, by purpose: `run/`, `data/`. Each script prints its options with `--help`.

| folder | what it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `run/` | runs on one card, each printing one JSON line and writing its record through `prism.check.harness`: `preset.py` (a preset of `configs/pipeline/` and its check; its options in [docs/guides/cli.md](../docs/guides/cli.md), "The preset runner"); `raster_frame.py` (`--scene` (default `tiny`) `[--view V] [--size W H] [--cores N] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: a whole frame by the raster class alone, stepped by the host, against the reference; the record to `measurements/raster_frame/` by default); `texture_frame.py` (`--scene` (default `sponza`) `--view` (default `overview`) `[--size W H] [--cores N] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: a shaded frame by the raster and texture classes alone, stepped by the host, against the reference's shaded frame; `measurements/texture_frame/` by default); `field.py` (`--scene S [--bounces N] [--updates N] [--stepped] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: the preset `field`, the indirect field's update alone, its check whole or stepped (the host's apply between the steps); `measurements/field_update/runs/` by default); `apply.py` (`--scene sponza\|bistro_interior [--calls N] [--frame-compute] [--card N] [--out FOLDER]`: the preset `apply`, the light-transport apply alone, `--frame-compute` made with the frame program's compute set; `measurements/apply/` by default). `--card` is 0 by default; an option left out is the preset's |
| `data/` | `generated_scenes.py OUT [NAME ...]` (writes the generated scenes, all four if none named, as `OUT/<name>.npz`, and prints their sizes and checksums); `public_scenes.py list\|fetch\|materials\|lighting\|indirect [SCENE ...] [--data FOLDER] [--workers N] [--rebake]` (the public scenes: list, download and convert, their materials, lighting and indirect files under `$PRISM_DATA` or `--data`; [docs/concepts/scenes.md](../docs/concepts/scenes.md)) |
